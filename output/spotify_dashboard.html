            Reading the numbers

            Notes

            Partial years

            The export starts on 14 May 2023 and ends on 27 Sep 2026, so 2023 and 2026 cover only part of a year.

            Plays vs. time

            A play counts when a track ran 30 seconds or more, the same rule Spotify uses. Rankings use total time, so long songs weigh more than short ones.

            Skips

            Spotify marks a track as skipped whenever you moved on before it ended, including a tap on the next button after a few seconds. That makes skip rates look high for everyone.

            Video plays included

            Music videos and video podcasts come in separate files. They are merged in here, and 91 duplicate rows were removed.
