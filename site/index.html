Genres and decades are tags I gave your 400 most-played artists (95% of your music time) based on what each artist is known for. Spotify's export has no genre data. Unknown artists with Hebrew song titles count as Israeli; the rest go to "Everything else".

Eras come from a segmentation that splits the timeline where the mix of genres and artists changed most. April–May 2026 had almost no listening and sits outside any era.

Variety is the number of artists that would give the same spread if your time were split evenly among them. Similarity compares which artists filled each month.
